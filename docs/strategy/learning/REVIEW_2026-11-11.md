## How to read this

- n is small. Nothing under n=10 is ever called EDGE or LEAK.
- R = net $ (fees, funding in) over the $ the opening stop risked at full size.
- Mean R and net $ CIs: seeded bootstrap, 2,000 resamples. p: seeded permutation test of the slice's mean R against every other trade (the all row: against 0).
- LEANING: p < 0.2, or under 10 trades all the same sign. FLUKE: dropping the biggest trade flips the sign. NOISE: the rest, and anything under 3 trades.
- Luck pct: where the trade's R sits among 500 random entries that UTC day, same side, stop and target distance, same holding time, on 5m bars.
