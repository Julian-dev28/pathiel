Privacy

pathiel collects nothing.

pathiel runs on your computer. There is no account system, no analytics and no telemetry, and the project runs no server that receives your data. This page says exactly what that means.

Keys

Your keys stay in ~/.pathiel/.env.local on your machine, created with permissions that only you can read. The API wallet key signs requests to Hyperliquid and is never printed, logged or sent anywhere but Hyperliquid. Your master wallet key is never stored by pathiel: connecting a wallet has your browser wallet sign one approval on a page served from your own machine (127.0.0.1), and the signature never leaves it.

What leaves your machine

  • Hyperliquid's API (mainnet or testnet): market and account reads, the orders you ask for, and the API wallet approval you sign.
  • Public data feeds, for research and the desk briefs: EIA, FRED, CFTC, Congress and the Federal Register, prediction markets (Polymarket, Kalshi), and news (Google News). An optional API key for one of these feeds goes only to that feed.
  • GitHub, only if you join the research pool: the signed statement your pull request carries.

Nothing else leaves your machine. No analytics script, no error reporter, no usage ping.

What stays on your machine

Theses, the desk journal, caches and your config stay under ~/.pathiel until you delete them. No pathiel process holds a withdrawal key: your master private key is read only at a hidden terminal prompt for the one action that needs it, and never from a file.

The research pool (opt-in)

Off by default. If you join, pathiel reads your address's already-public Hyperliquid fills on the pool coins and publishes a graded summary at least 24 hours late: your address, the date you joined, your trade counts, and a win rate, mean result, label and luck score. It never publishes your balance, open positions, single trade prices or sizes, or any key. Leaving removes your rows from the next build. Full terms: docs/PRIVACY.md.

Coming next (0.9)

A keyless research server is built and lands in the next release: macro series, the event calendar, headlines and prediction-market odds, with no account and no key required to read it.

Questions or a problem

Open a GitHub issue, or report a security problem privately through a security advisory. More detail on how keys are handled: SECURITY.md.
