Copycat

Mirror up to 10 Hyperliquid wallets at your leverage and your size.
How it works, and what can go wrong

Every cycle the loop reads each leader's positions. When a leader opens, adds, reduces, flips or closes, the book does the same on your account. One leader owns a coin at a time: the first to open it. Exits are mirrored even when copying is off, so nothing is left orphaned.

No stops. Copies are exempt from the daily-loss flatten and the DSL exits, because a stop the leader doesn't have would break the mirror. The daily-loss gate still blocks new opens. At max isolated leverage a copy liquidates on a move of roughly 1/leverage: 40x is about 2.5% minus maintenance margin.

Untested. There is no backtest of this 10-wallet rule. W-CP1 backtested copying 0xe282 and refuted it.
