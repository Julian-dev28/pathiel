Get started

Point pathiel at your Hyperliquid account

Create an agent wallet in the Hyperliquid app (More, API), put its address and key plus your main address in .env.local (the plugin reads ~/.pathiel/.env.local; /pathiel:setup walks you through it), then restart. An agent key can trade and can never withdraw; the master key stays in your wallet. This page picks up the account on its next load.
